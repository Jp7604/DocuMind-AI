# Terms of Service (Draft)

This document is a draft and should be reviewed before commercial deployment.

DocuMind AI allows users to upload PDF documents and ask questions about their contents. Uploaded files are processed to extract text, create embeddings, retrieve relevant passages, and generate answers using the configured AI provider.

By using the service, you agree to:
- upload only documents that you have the right to process and query;
- not use the system for unlawful, abusive, or harmful activity;
- keep your API credentials confidential and not share them publicly.

DocuMind AI does not guarantee that answers are always accurate. Source-backed answers may still be incomplete, outdated, or context-limited. Users should verify any business, legal, or operational decision against the original source material.

This draft is not a final legal commitment and should be reviewed by counsel before launch.
